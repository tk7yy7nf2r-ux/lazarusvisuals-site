Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

LazarusVisuals is a creator workflow used to organize, schedule and publish original social media content through supported third-party platforms.

1. Information processed

When a user connects a supported social account, LazarusVisuals may process authorization tokens, account identifiers, profile information made available by the platform, and publishing data required to provide the requested functionality.

2. Purpose of processing

Information is used only to authenticate connected accounts, display relevant account information, schedule content, publish user-selected content, and operate the creator workflow.

3. Storage

The current LazarusVisuals workflow is designed to be self-hosted. Data may be stored on infrastructure controlled by the operator of the installation. Third-party platforms may also process data according to their own privacy policies.

4. Sharing

LazarusVisuals does not sell personal information. Data is shared with connected platforms only when necessary to authenticate an account or perform actions requested by the user, such as publishing content.

5. Retention and disconnection

Authorization data may be retained while an integration remains connected. Users can revoke platform access through the relevant social platform and may remove locally stored integration data from the self-hosted installation.

6. Security

Reasonable technical measures should be used to protect access credentials and authorization tokens. Users are responsible for securing the device and infrastructure on which the self-hosted service runs.

7. Third-party services

Connected services such as TikTok operate under their own terms and privacy policies. LazarusVisuals does not control those third-party services.

8. Updates

This policy may be updated as the service or its integrations evolve. The latest version will remain available on this page.
